Mindframe Publisher Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Request deletion

Email jcawtsdemo@gmail.com with the subject: Mindframe Publisher Data Deletion Request.

Include the Facebook Page name or Page ID associated with your request and describe the information you want deleted. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or app secrets.

How requests are handled

We will verify your authority over the relevant information, identify applicable local files and Airtable records, and respond with the outcome or any additional information required.

Deletion may include stored Page information, publication records, associated media files, workflow execution records, and authorization credentials, as applicable.

Backup copies may remain until the relevant backups are replaced or removed.

Revoke access

Removing stored credentials stops the tool from using those credentials. You can also revoke the app's access through Facebook's app or business integration settings.

Published Facebook content

Published Facebook posts are separate from the tool's stored records. Tell us explicitly if your request also concerns removal of published content from a Page you control.
