Mindframe Publisher Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Mindframe Publisher is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the Mindframe Moments Facebook Page. It prepares and publishes original videos to that Page.

Information used

The tool uses Facebook authorization credentials, Page identifiers and names, video and post identifiers, upload and publication statuses, and content prepared for publication.

Its current workflow does not request viewers' private messages, friends lists, or personal profiles, and does not retrieve viewer comments.

Purpose

Information is used to identify the authorized Page, upload videos, publish Reels, check processing and publication status, and maintain production records.

Storage and service providers

Production records are stored in Airtable. Media files and workflow execution records are stored on the operator's local computer through n8n. Authorization credentials are stored in n8n's credential system.

Meta receives videos, descriptions, and API requests needed for Facebook publication. Airtable receives production and publication records. These services process information under their own policies.

We do not sell information obtained through the app.

Retention and deletion

Production and publication records are retained while needed to operate the Page and troubleshoot the workflow. Authorization credentials are retained while the integration remains authorized.

You may request deletion of information held by the operator by emailing jcawtsdemo@gmail.com. Requests will be verified before action is taken. Copies retained in backups may remain until those backups are replaced or removed.

Deleting local records does not automatically remove content already published on Facebook. Removal of Facebook content must be handled separately through the Page or Meta.

Contact and changes

Contact: jcawtsdemo@gmail.com

This policy will be updated if the app's data use changes.
